Signed load calculation questions

Can I use a homeowner permit for EV charger wiring?

Calgary excludes EV charger wiring and electric vehicle energy management systems (EVEMS) from homeowner electrical permits. A registered electrical contractor must obtain a permit for that installation. Where a load calculation is required, a qualified electrician must prepare it.

Does every EV charger project need the same paperwork?

The City lists different requirements for detached homes, shared services and energy management equipment. Tell us your home type, the proposed equipment and any instructions you received from the City so we can identify the documents for your project.

Do you have to come to my house?

That depends on the information available. Photos, equipment ratings and a video call may be enough; a site visit may be needed if details cannot be confirmed remotely.

When will the signed calculation be ready?

Send your deadline with the project details. Real World Electric will confirm timing after checking the records and whether a site visit is needed.

What if the calculation shows my service is too small?

We can discuss equipment choices, suitable load management or a service upgrade. The options depend on the service and the loads you plan to add.
