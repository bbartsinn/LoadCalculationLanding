The City of Calgary Load Calculation Form, Explained

Real World Electric · Updated 3 November 2026

Get the current worksheet

Download the City of Calgary load calculation worksheet from the City's secondary suite guidance. Check the current form and submission requirements for your permit. The worksheet documents the calculation; it does not replace a permit or the rest of a project's design information.

Check which submission applies

The secondary suite process asks for the calculation before electrical rough-in. Other projects, including EV charging with an energy management system, can have different requirements. Use the City's electrical permit guidance for the relevant project type.

Complete the fields in order

Project address and permit numbers

Identify the main dwelling and each suite, using the correct addresses and electrical permit numbers. Check that these match the permit records and project drawings.

Dwelling areas

Enter area in square metres. To convert square feet, divide by 10.764: for example, 1,200 ft² is about 111.5 m². Identify above-ground and basement areas separately, and confirm which area is included under the applicable rule.

Basic area load

Living areaBasic load
First 90 m²5,000 W
Each additional 90 m², or portion of it1,000 W

For example, 95 m² of applicable living area gives a 6,000 W basic area load. The extra 5 m² starts another block; it is not prorated. This is one component of the calculation, not the total service requirement.

Heating and cooling

Record the equipment ratings. If a non-simultaneous allowance is used, confirm that the installation meets its conditions. Do not assume heating and cooling cannot operate together merely because they are usually used in different seasons.

Ranges and water heating

Include each dwelling's range. Identify water heaters as storage-tank or tankless, because their demand treatment differs. Use the equipment nameplates and the worksheet's categories.

EV charging, pools, hot tubs and spas

Record the relevant equipment ratings in their designated rows. Where an EVEMS is proposed, check the City's current equipment and submission requirements separately. Do not manually reduce the charger rating without an applicable basis.

Additional fixed loads

List other applicable fixed loads, such as dryers and storage-tank water heaters. Do not count equipment here if it has already been included in heating, cooling, EV charging or another dedicated category. Apply the relevant demand treatment rather than one percentage to every appliance.

Totals, breaker and conductors

Check the calculated watts and amps, then the service breaker and conductor entries. Record the actual or proposed service accurately. A calculator's suggested size should not be copied into an existing-service field without verification.

Minimum service provisions also matter

The worksheet lists 100 A for a single dwelling with at least 80 m² excluding basement, and 60 A below that area. Check how these provisions apply alongside calculated demand and the proposed supply arrangement.

Declaration and signature

Complete the declaration and required identification on the current form. Confirm who must prepare or sign it for your permit type; homeowner and contractor applications do not have identical eligibility requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I get the City of Calgary load calculation form?

Download the current worksheet from the City and check the submission instructions for your project.

When does the secondary suite calculation have to be submitted?

Calgary asks for it before the electrical rough-in inspection. Prepare it earlier so any capacity or documentation issues can be addressed.

Is this form required for every electrical permit?

No single requirement covers every project. Check the City electrical permit guidance for the type of work and any energy management system involved.

Can I submit the calculator PDF without checking it?

Check the exported form against the equipment, drawings and permit details first. Complete any required declaration and signature; generating a PDF does not establish permit acceptance.

Planning information only. Confirm the calculation, equipment and current submission requirements for your project. Final acceptance rests with the authority having jurisdiction.
