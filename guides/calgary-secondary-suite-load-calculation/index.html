Calgary Secondary Suite Electrical Load Calculation

Real World Electric · Updated 3 November 2026

What Calgary asks for

Calgary asks for a secondary suite electrical load calculation before the electrical rough-in inspection. The worksheet records the dwelling areas, equipment loads and service details. Complete it early enough to address any capacity or equipment changes before construction.

Use the City's secondary suite requirements and current load calculation worksheet. Follow the City's submission instructions and include the relevant permit numbers and addresses.

Gather the inputs for both dwellings

  • Floor areas in square metres, with above-ground and basement areas identified separately.
  • Nameplate ratings for heating, cooling, ranges, water heaters, EV charging and any pool, hot tub or spa equipment.
  • Other fixed loads, including dryers and storage-tank water heaters.
  • The existing service rating and the equipment proposed for the suite.

Use equipment ratings and project drawings rather than estimates from the number of breakers. If the service or conductor details are unclear, have an electrician identify them.

How the calculation is organised

1. Basic load from floor area

The dwelling calculation under CEC Rule 8-200 starts with a basic area allowance. Determine the applicable living area under Rule 8-110; do not substitute the total area from a property listing without checking what it includes.

Living areaBasic load
First 90 m²5,000 W
Each additional 90 m², or portion of it1,000 W

2. Equipment loads

Add each dwelling's equipment using the applicable demand rules. Ranges, heating and cooling, EV charging and water-heating equipment do not all use the same treatment. Keep each load in the correct category and count it once.

Only use a heating/cooling non-simultaneous allowance where the installation meets the applicable conditions. A label saying that equipment is interlocked needs to reflect how it actually operates.

3. Combined service demand

The shared-service calculation applies the relevant multi-unit demand factors and separately accounts for loads excluded from those factors. It is not simply the sum of appliance ratings, nor a blanket discount on every load. The City's worksheet identifies the applicable rule references.

Check service minimums separately

The City's worksheet lists a 100 A minimum for a single dwelling with at least 80 m² excluding basement, and 60 A below that area. These minimum provisions and the calculated demand both need to be considered for the proposed arrangement. An existing service requires a project-specific assessment.

If the existing service is insufficient

  • Review equipment choices. Different heating, cooking or water-heating equipment can change demand. Compare the complete installation implications before changing a specification.
  • Assess load management. A suitable EVEMS may be an option where EV charging adds demand. Equipment acceptance and installation requirements need separate review.
  • Plan a service upgrade. Confirm utility capacity and the service-change process early if an upgrade is needed.

For the capacity question, see whether a 100 A service can support a suite. For the paperwork, use the Calgary form walkthrough.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Calgary secondary suite load calculation due?

Calgary asks for it before the electrical rough-in inspection. Prepare it during planning so any required changes can be addressed before construction.

Who completes and signs the form?

Follow the current City form and the requirements for your permit. The permit applicant is responsible for providing the required information; do not assume homeowner and contractor applications have identical eligibility or signature requirements.

Does a secondary suite always require a service upgrade?

No. The answer depends on the calculated demand, the existing service and the proposed equipment. The number of dwelling units alone does not decide it.

Does this also cover laneway or backyard suites?

The City worksheet includes laneway/backyard suite fields. Confirm the supply arrangement and the applicable requirements for a detached dwelling before using the calculation.

Planning information only. Confirm the calculation, equipment and current submission requirements for your project. Final acceptance rests with the authority having jurisdiction.
