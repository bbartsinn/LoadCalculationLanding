Need a signed load calculation?

A signed residential load calculation starts at $120 CAD online or $250 CAD for a Calgary site visit. Installation is quoted separately.

Choose “New car charger” on the assessment form. For your own estimate in watts and amps, try the residential electrical load calculator. The estimate is optional.

Can I charge on my existing service?

Choose a charging rate

We check how the proposed charger fits alongside your appliances and heating. A lower charging rate may suit a short daily commute and overnight parking.

Manage the charging load

When capacity is tight, a suitable energy management system can reduce or pause charging while the house uses more power.

Consider a service upgrade

If your existing service cannot support the proposed charging rate and other planned loads, we can explain the upgrade work and the details to confirm with ENMAX.

What to include in your request

Send what you know. You can ask about an installation before you’ve picked a charger.

  • Your Calgary neighbourhood, the type of home and where you park.
  • The charger model if you have one, your usual daily driving distance and how long you park at home.
  • For a load review, mention major electrical equipment and planned additions such as a heat pump or suite.
  • If you email a photo of the panel, leave all covers in place.

Who gets the EV charger permit?

In Calgary, a licensed electrical contractor obtains the permit for EV charging wiring and EV energy management installations. If you have a shared service in a townhouse or condo, the building’s capacity and the board’s requirements matter too.
