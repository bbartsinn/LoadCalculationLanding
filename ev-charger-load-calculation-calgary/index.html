What can work on your existing service?

Charge at the rate you need

A shorter commute and a full night parked at home may leave room to choose a lower charging rate. I’ll compare the proposed charger with your other electrical loads and the time your car has to charge.

Share available capacity

An EV energy management system can reduce or pause charging while the house needs more power. If the calculation shows limited capacity, we can check whether managed charging suits your home and the equipment you want.

Price a service upgrade

If your preferred charging rate and other planned loads exceed what the existing service can supply, we can look at the scope of an upgrade and what needs confirmation with ENMAX.

ENMAX’s guide to charging at home covers charger choices and service capacity. For a closer look at the calculation, read our Calgary EV charger load guide.

What to send Bart

A few details help me determine whether I can assess the service remotely or need to see it in person. Send what you have.

  • Your Calgary neighbourhood, type of home and where the vehicle parks.
  • A photo of the closed panel and any service rating visible from outside. Leave all covers in place.
  • The charger model, if you've picked one, your usual daily driving distance and how long the car stays parked.
  • Major electrical loads at home and anything you expect to add, such as a heat pump, hot tub or secondary suite.

Who handles the EV charger permit?

In Calgary, a licensed electrical contractor obtains the permit for EV charging wiring and EV energy management installations. If you live in a townhouse or condo with a shared service, the building’s capacity and your board’s requirements can also affect the project.

Read the City of Calgary’s homeowner permit and electrification guidance.

If you need a signed residential load calculation, remote reviews start at $120 CAD and Calgary site visits start at $250 CAD. Those prices cover the calculation service. Installation is quoted separately.
