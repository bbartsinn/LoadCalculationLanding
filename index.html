Canadian Electrical Code

Section 8.
Loading and demand.

Electrical demand is more than the sum of the numbers on your breakers. Section 8 of the Canadian Electrical Code sets out circuit-loading and demand-factor rules used when assessing service capacity.

Calgary's secondary-suite worksheet references Rule 8-200 and Rule 8-202 for dwelling loads and combined demand. The calculator provides a planning estimate; the applicable rules and your project's service requirements still need a qualified review.

Before you calculate

What does an electrical load calculation tell me?

It estimates the demand used to assess a property's electrical service. Floor area, equipment ratings and applicable demand factors all matter. It is not simply the total of the breaker ratings or the equipment's connected load.

Which electrical code applies in Alberta?

Alberta currently uses the 2024 Canadian Electrical Code, CSA C22.1-24 (26th edition), in force since April 1, 2025. Confirm the requirements that apply to your project with the receiving authority.

Can I use the exported form for a permit?

Check the current City form requirements and have the calculation reviewed before submission. Recalculate after editing inputs and check the exported values. The app does not provide a signature or permit approval.

Does the calculator cover commercial systems or EV load management?

The calculator is for residential planning. It does not model EV energy-management systems. Commercial distribution, solar interconnection and other system designs need a separate assessment.
